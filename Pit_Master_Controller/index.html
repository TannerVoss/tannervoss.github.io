Pit Master Controller is an independent Android companion for compatible Pit Boss smoker controllers. Keep temperatures, supported controls, cooking tools and alerts together in a dashboard that gets straight to the point.

Less app wrestling. More cooking.

Check reported grill and probe temperatures, adjust supported temperature settings, and keep track of your cook without digging through a maze of screens. Local Wi-Fi communication keeps your phone talking to the smoker on your network; Bluetooth supports discovery and setup on compatible controllers.

  • Grill and probe readings in one clear dashboard.
  • Cooking timers, temperature history and configurable high/low alerts.
  • Saved grill profiles and diagnostic exports to help troubleshoot connection issues.
  • Optional background monitoring with a visible Android notification.

The creator’s verdict after hands-on Lockhart testing: it has performed amazingly well and feels miles ahead of the Pit Boss app. This is the experience that drove the project—not an independent benchmark or a promise for every smoker.

Tested on the Lockhart. Tell us about yours.

Hardware testing has been exclusively on the Lockhart. Compatibility with other models and firmware is not confirmed. Available readings, controls and setup options depend on your smoker’s controller and firmware.

Help make it better: send feedback, connection issues and feature ideas to pitmastercontrollersupporter@gmail.com. Include your smoker’s model name, your Android phone model, what you tried and what happened. Include controller firmware if you know it.

A few things to know

Published by Tanner Voss. Pit Master Controller is independently developed and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pit Boss, Dansons, or any appliance manufacturer. Product names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.

The app retains Google advertising and consent integration for ad-supported use. Banner loading and display are disabled in the trial. See the privacy policy for advertising data practices and privacy choices.

Your phone and smoker must be reachable on the same local network for Wi-Fi control. Compatible Wi-Fi setup requires a 2.4 GHz network. Bluetooth and notification permissions are needed for features that use them. Android may limit background monitoring; alerts are not guaranteed.

Always supervise an operating smoker and follow the manufacturer’s instructions. This app is not a safety system, fire alarm, or substitute for checking food with an appropriate thermometer. Never expose the smoker’s control interface to the public internet.
