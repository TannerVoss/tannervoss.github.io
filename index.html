Pit Master Controller

An independent Android companion app designed for compatible smoker controllers, including the Pit Boss Lockhart PB2180LK.

Local Wi-Fi and Bluetooth connections, a smoker dashboard, cooking tools and alerts. Availability depends on controller model and firmware. The app contains banner ads.

Published by Tanner Voss. Pit Master Controller is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pit Boss or its manufacturer. Never leave an operating smoker unattended or rely on this app as a safety system.

Android testing

The first closed test has been submitted to Google Play for review. Downloads will become available to eligible testers after approval.
